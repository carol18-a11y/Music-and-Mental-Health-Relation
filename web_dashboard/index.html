⚖️ Welch Two-Sample t-Test (Workplace Music Effect)

t.test(Anxiety ~ While.working)

Research Question: Does listening to music while working significantly impact anxiety levels?

Null Hypothesis (H₀): μYes - μNo = 0
Alternative (H₁): μYes - μNo ≠ 0 (Two-tailed)

🧠 Bayes' Theorem: Posterior Probabilities $P(\text{Genre} \mid \text{High Anxiety})$
